Hello, I'm

Suzy the Wanderer

Travel writer · Food chaser · Lisbon-based

I grew up in Lisbon, in a pastel-pink building where the smell of fresh pastéis de nata drifted up from the bakery downstairs every morning. Watching the ferries cross the Tagus at golden hour, I always wondered where they were headed next. A couple of years ago I finally stopped wondering — I packed one backpack, bought a one-way ticket, and promised myself I'd say yes to every street-food stall, overnight train and wrong turn that came my way.

Since then I've wandered through Tokyo's neon-lit alleys, haggled my way around the souks of Marrakech, and eaten more bowls of pho in Hanoi than I'd care to admit. This blog is where I share the places, plates and people that stayed with me — honest stories, practical tips and the occasional sunset I couldn't stop photographing. Lisbon is still home base, but the whole world feels a little more like home now. I hope these stories help you find your own corner of it.

— Suzy
